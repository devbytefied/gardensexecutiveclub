Core Group

Core is The Garden's Executive Club's critically acclaimed peer group experience. This curated peer group of 10 career contemporaries forges deep connection, camaraderie, and support. Core groups convene monthly to confidentially work through challenges with a dedicated Core guide — a professional facilitator experienced in executive leadership development.

Salon Series

The Garden's Executive Club’s virtual events are designed to connect and inspire. Sessions include conversations with industry icons and workshops designed for senior leaders to sharpen their knives.

Connection

The Garden's Executive Club facilitates strong connections between leaders across industries. Members keep in close contact with their Core group and network with the entire community.
